## Trigger

Understand academic evidence, evaluate research quality, or survey field progress through peer-reviewed literature.

## Do not use when

Task can be adequately answered by the technical deep-dive route; or the decision burden is product selection, vendor choice, or market entry (attach academic evidence discipline instead).

## Artifact contract

Scope and search strategy, key themes and trends, major breakthroughs, state of the art, open questions, evidence quality assessment; field-progress style adds opening verdict, mechanism/benchmark ladder, representative failure cases, conditions that would change the conclusion.

## Failure signs (hard-fail keywords)

- `preprints as peer-reviewed`
- `cherry-picked papers`
- `causation from observation`
- `p-values without effect sizes`
- `outdated research`
- `unlabeled venue status`
- `ignored publication bias`
